Quick tip3 AXI handshake bugs I keep findingFri Jul 3
Body text (ready to post)
3 AXI VALID/READY bugs I've found in real testbenches 👇 1/ VALID waits for READY. The spec says VALID must NOT depend on READY. The moment your master checks READY before asserting VALID, you can deadlock — and it'll only show under backpressure. 2/ Deasserting VALID before the handshake completes. Once VALID is high it must stay high (with stable payload) until the cycle where VALID && READY are both 1. Drop it early and you've dropped a transaction the scoreboard still expects. 3/ Ignoring the B channel. Plenty of write tests fire AW + W and never check B. A slave returning SLVERR silently passes — until silicon. All three hide under light traffic and only bite under randomized backpressure. That's why your AXI VIP needs ready-delay randomization built in. Which of these has bitten you? (I've shipped #2 myself.) 👇 Mentoring on AXI/UVM + DV interviews → topmate.io/cshubham #SystemVerilog #DesignVerification #VLSI #AMBA #AXI
